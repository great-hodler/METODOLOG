# START HERE — The One Skill That's Going Up in Price

*Intro lesson · watch-along · ~7 min · the only "homework" is one sentence*

## LAYER 1 — TEXT (lesson / video script)

### The headline that should wake you up

Last year a guy named Matthew Gallagher sat in a house in Los Angeles with $20,000 and no team. No office. No investors. Just him, his brother, and about a dozen AI tools.

In his first full year, his company did $401 million in sales. The New York Times checked the numbers. They're real.

He didn't write most of the code — AI did. He didn't hire a marketing team — AI wrote the copy and made the ads. Customer support? Also AI. Two people. Four hundred million dollars.

Sam Altman, the guy who runs OpenAI, called this back in 2024. He said a one-person, billion-dollar company was coming. People laughed. Then it showed up.

**Now — I'm not going to promise you $400 million.** That would be a lie, and you'd close this video on the spot. Good instinct, by the way. Keep it.

But here's what's true for *you*: the exact tools Matthew used are sitting right there, open to you, today. And you don't need his goal. At your level — your business, your hours — an extra one or ten thousand dollars a month isn't a fantasy. It's something we build together in this course. On real examples. Things I built myself, tested myself, and made money with. No theory. No made-up screenshots.

That's the whole course.

---

### First, who's talking

I'm **Nia Leon**. A few years ago, I was you.

I had a business of one. I was the marketer, the copywriter, the support team, the bookkeeper, the salesperson, and the assistant. Six jobs. One salary — mine. I worked 60-hour weeks and answered DMs at 11 at night with 16 tabs open and cold coffee next to me.

I almost quit. Twice.

Then I stopped trying to do *more* and started building a team that wasn't human. Today I run my business in about an hour a day. Same business. Same me. The only thing that changed is what's doing the work.

I'm not a coder. I'm not a "tech person." I'm someone who got tired enough to find a better way — and I'm going to hand you the exact one I use.

---

### The way you work now (and why it's breaking)

Right now you wear all the hats. Every task in your business has exactly one employee: you.

So everything waits on you. The post doesn't get written until you write it. The DM doesn't get answered until you answer it. The invoice doesn't go out until you — at 11 p.m. — finally remember it.

That's not a business. That's a job you can't quit, with a boss who never lets up. You.

You hired yourself for six roles and you're paying yourself for none of them. We're going to fix the hiring.

---

### The way it works now

Most people use AI like a vending machine. Type a question, grab the answer, walk away. That's like hiring a five-star chef and only asking him what time it is. Huge waste.

Here's the better picture. Imagine five employees. A copywriter. A designer. A marketer. An assistant. A salesperson. They all know your business cold. They know your voice. They never call in sick, never vanish for two weeks in August, and never ask for a raise.

**That's an AI Dream Team.** Not a chatbot you forget about — an actual team where each one has a role and gets the work done while you go make coffee.

It's exactly what Matthew did. He didn't hire people. He gave each job to AI: the coder, the copywriter, the designer, the support rep. Same move. Smaller scale. Your business.

---

### Why this is the one skill worth learning right now

Be honest with yourself for a second. The skills you can buy on the open market keep getting cheaper. Writing an offer. Setting up an ad. Designing a post. AI does all of those now, for almost nothing. The price of every one of those skills is falling. Fast.

One skill is going the other way. **Up.**

The skill of building AI to do the work *for* you. Of turning a task you hate into an employee that handles it. That one gets more valuable every month — because the people who have it run circles around the people who don't.

This is the part I want you to sit with. The people who learn to build AI teams won't just save time. They'll out-work, out-ship, and out-earn the ones who keep doing it all by hand — until those people quietly drop off the map.

I'm not saying this to scare you. I'm saying it because **you're early.** Most of your competitors haven't figured this out yet. That gap is your head start. We're going to use it.

---

### What this looks like in real money

Not theory. My numbers.

We used to pay a designer $500 to $1,000 a month. Explain the task three times, wait four days, get back something wrong, explain again. AI does the same work on the first pass — and reads what I mean better than most people did. Three months in, we'd kept over **$3,000** in the business. From one role.

Our ad employee built the creatives, tested them, and ran the campaigns. Over eight months that brought in about **$160,000** on cold traffic in the US. The carousels we made brought in another **$5,000-plus** in organic sales on top.

This isn't a story I read somewhere. This is a normal Tuesday now.

---

### The path we'll walk — three steps

You don't get there by collecting clever questions to ask a chatbot. You get there by building, in order, three things.

**1. Your Second Brain.** First you load your business into AI — once. Who you are, how you talk, what you sell, who you serve. From then on, it knows you. No more re-explaining yourself every morning like you're talking to a stranger.

**2. Your AI Dream Team.** Then you build the employees. Content, marketing, sales, operations — real roles, doing real work, in your voice. You stop managing a to-do list. You hand work to a team.

**3. Your One-Person Company.** Finally it all runs from your phone. You talk, it works. You stop being the person who does everything and become the person who *decides* everything. About an hour a day.

That's the whole map. Second Brain, then the Team, then the Company in your pocket. We build it piece by piece — and you'll have something working in the **first hour.**

---

### About the tool — let me be straight with you

We use **Claude** in this course. Not ChatGPT, not Gemini — Claude.

Here's the honest part. You could build most of this on ChatGPT or Gemini too. The thinking you'll learn works anywhere.

And we're not paid to say "Claude." No affiliate deal. No partner links. No kickback. We honestly don't care which company you give your money to. We care about three things: does it actually work, does it make your life easier, and is it simple enough for someone who isn't technical. Today, for building a team of AI employees, Claude wins on all three. That's the only reason it's here. If that changes, we'll change.

So you're not learning "Claude buttons." You're learning how to build AI employees. The skill comes with you, wherever AI goes next.

---

### You don't need to be technical. At all.

I'll say it plainly, because I know it's the fear: **if you can record a voice note, you can do this.** No code. No API keys. No setup that needs a 22-year-old to explain it to you. I get stuck on tech too — so everywhere this can trip you up, I've already put the fix right there in the lesson. You won't be left guessing.

Here's the line I want you to remember:

> **Every hour you spend building these systems saves you a hundred hours of work down the road.**

That's the trade. An hour of setup now buys back your evenings later. Which is why I'm going to ask something of you.

Treat this course like it's the most important thing you'll learn this year. Block the time. Protect it like a paying client's call. Of everything on your plate, this is the one that pays you back the most — it's what lets you pull ahead while everyone else is still asking a chatbot one question at a time. Bet on it. You won't regret it.

---

### One thing before you go

Grab your phone or a scrap of paper. Write down **one task that drives you crazy.** The one you keep doing yourself every week even though you hate it. The one you've meant to hand off for months but couldn't afford to, or couldn't find the right person for.

One task. One sentence. Right now. Don't overthink it.

That task is your **first AI employee.** We build it together in Module 1 — and it becomes your proof that this whole thing actually works.

Welcome to your AI Dream Team. Let's get to work.
