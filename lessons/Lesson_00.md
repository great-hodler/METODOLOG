## LAYER 1 — TEXT (lesson / video script)

Let me guess. It's late. The house is finally quiet. You've got 16 tabs open, your coffee's gone cold, and your real workday is just starting. The posts, the DMs, the invoices. Some nights it feels like you hired yourself for six different jobs… and you're paying yourself for none of them.

And maybe you've already tried all the AI things? You copied the "magic prompts." You watched the YouTube tutorials. You paid for the subscriptions. And whatever came out still sounded like an AI and you still ended up rewriting everything yourself.

I'm Nia. And in this course, we're going to fix that — for real this time.

Just a year ago, I was, probably, where you are now. Burned out. Sixty-hour weeks. Wearing every hat and honestly about ready to quit. But today I run my whole business in about an hour a day. My content, my newsletters, my client replies, my landing pages — every repetitive task has become an AI employee. I've got around fifty of them working in the background right now. Every 24 hours they quietly handle hundreds of little jobs. Without me.

And I'm not a tech person or an AI guru. I just got tired enough to find a better way, and I'm handing you the exact system I use.

So here's what we're really doing here. We are not fixing your to-do list. We're fixing the actual problem underneath it: right now, you are the entire team.

Look — most people use AI like a vending machine. You type a question, grab the answer, walk away. It advises you. It hands you a draft, a list, an idea… and then you still do the work. You rewrite it so it sounds like you. You paste it where it goes. You're still the one at the keyboard at 11 PM. All AI did was turn you into a slightly faster bottleneck.

Here's the whole shift, in one line.
Old way: AI tells you how.
New way: AI just does it.

It makes the Instagram carousels.
It writes the email replies.
It reads your Stripe reports and tells you what actually matters.
It chases the follow-ups you always forget.

That's the move this whole course makes — you stop being the one who does the work, and become the one who decides. The work gets done by a team that knows your business, talks in your voice, and works to grow it.

**And here is how we are going to get there. Three simple steps:**

**Step one: your Second Brain.**
We load your business and your voice into AI, one time. Who you are, how you talk, what you sell, who you serve. From then on, it just knows you — no more re-explaining yourself every single morning like you're talking to a stranger.

**Step two: your AI Dream Team.**
Now we build the employees. Content, marketing, sales, operations — real roles, doing real work. This is where you stop managing a to-do list and start handing work to a team.

**And step three: your One-Person Company.**
This is the fun part — you learn to run the whole thing from your phone. You stop being the person who does everything, and become the person who decides everything.

**The platform we use to build your AI Dream Team is Claude.**
Today it's simply the easiest place to build a team that remembers your business and really sounds like you. Here let me be straight with you. I have no affiliate deal, no partner links, no kickback. You could build most of this on ChatGPT or Gemini too. The method doesn't care. Because we're not learning "Claude buttons" — we're learning how to build AI employees. That skill comes with you, wherever AI goes next.

**Okay. Now I have to be honest with you about something bigger.**
Almost every skill you can pay for is getting cheaper by the month. Copywriting. Design. Running ads. AI does all of it now, for next to nothing. There's only ONE skill moving the other way — going up in value — and this is it: taking a task you hate and turning it into an employee that just handles it for you.

This isn't "hype" anymore. It's quietly becoming a survival skill. The solopreneurs who pick it up now are the ones who pull ahead — and here's the good part: most of your competitors haven't figured this out yet. That gap, right now, is your head start. The best time to build your team was a year ago. The second best time is today.

Let me leave you with one story before you go.

Last year a guy named Matthew Gallagher started a company out of his LA apartment — zero employees. He built the whole thing — the website, the ads, the copy, the support — on a stack of AI tools. First full year: $401 million in sales. The whole "team" was him and his brother. Two people did the work that used to take two hundred. And the exact tools that made that possible? They're sitting right there, open to you, today.

I'm not promising you $401 million. But here's the line I really want you to remember:
Every hour you spend building these systems saves you a hundred hours of work later. One hour now buys back your evenings for months.

So here's my one ask. Block the time this weekend — protect it like a paying client's call. Because of everything on your plate, this is the one thing that buys back all the rest. I wouldn't ask if I didn't mean it.

And one tiny thing before you head into the next lesson. Grab your phone and write down one task that drives you crazy. The one you keep doing every week even though you hate it. Just one. One sentence. Don't overthink it. That's your very first hire. And it's exactly what we build together next.

Let's get to work.
