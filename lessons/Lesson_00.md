# Lesson 0 — The Last Course You'll Actually Finish

*Intro · watch-along · ~9 min · builds nothing · the only "homework" is one sentence*

## LAYER 1 — TEXT (lesson / video script)

### Beat 1 — Why you're here

It's 11pm. The kids are finally down. And now your *real* workday starts — the posts, the DMs, the invoices nobody else is going to touch.

You didn't start a business to work six jobs for one paycheck.

So tonight we're not fixing your to-do list. We're fixing the actual problem underneath it: **you are the entire team.** Every task in your business has exactly one employee. You.

Let's change that.

Honest time: about 9 minutes. This lesson builds nothing — no setup, no clicking, no homework except one sentence at the end. It's the 9 minutes where you see what we're about to build together, and decide if it's worth your weekend.

---

### Beat 2 — What you walk out with

You won't touch Claude today. That starts next lesson.

What you walk out with tonight is two things: the whole map of what we build, and your **first employee — named.** By the last line you'll have written one sentence that becomes the first thing your AI team takes off your plate.

That's it. Watch, then choose.

---

### Beat 3 — The shift (old way → new way)

Here's the trap you're in. You do everything — marketing, writing, support, sales, admin. All you.

So when you bring in AI, look at what actually happens. It *advises* you. It hands you a draft, a list, an idea. And then… you still do the work. You rewrite it so it sounds like you. You paste it where it goes. You're still the one at the keyboard at 11pm. AI just made you a slightly faster bottleneck.

One line says the whole shift:

> **Old: AI tells you how. New: AI just does it.**

That's the move this entire course makes. You stop being the one who does the work. You become the one who *decides*. The work gets done by a team you built.

And I mean a team. Picture five employees: a copywriter, a designer, a marketer, an assistant, a salesperson. They know your business cold. They know your voice. They never call in sick, never vanish for two weeks in August, never ask for a raise six months in.

That's an **AI Dream Team.** Not a chatbot you type a question into and forget. An actual team where each one has a role and gets the job done while you go make coffee.

Most people never get there, because most people use AI like a vending machine. Put in a question, grab what drops out, walk away. That's like hiring a five-star chef and only asking him what time it is. The chef can cook. You're standing there checking your watch.

---

### Beat 4 — Who's talking, and the map

Quick — who's saying all this.

I'm **Nia.** A few years ago, I was you. Two years of burnout. Six jobs, one salary — mine. Laptop open at 11pm, 16 tabs, running on coffee and guilt. I almost quit. Twice.

I'm not a guru and I don't want to be. I'm not a coder. I'm someone who got tired enough to find a better way — and what got me out is the exact team I'm handing you. Today I run the same business in about an hour a day. Same business. Same me. The only thing that changed is what's doing the work.

So here's my promise, and it's the whole job:

> **I'm not going to teach you AI. I'm going to hand you the exact team I use to run my business — and show you where to click. That's it.**

Here's the map. Three steps, in this order.

**1. Your Second Brain.** You load yourself and your business into Claude — once. Who you are, how you talk, what you sell, who you serve. From then on it knows you. No more re-explaining yourself every morning like you're talking to a stranger.

**2. Your AI Dream Team.** Then you hire the employees. Content, marketing, sales, operations — posts written, DMs answered, invoices chased, in your voice. You stop managing a to-do list. You hand work to a team.

**3. Your One-Person Company.** Finally it all runs from your phone. You talk, it works. You stop being the person who does everything and become the person who *decides* everything. About an hour a day.

Brain, team, company. That's the map — and you'll have something working in the **first hour**, not someday.

---

### Beat 5 — Proof (this is real, and not just me)

I won't show you fake screenshots. Here are real numbers — mine first, the small kind.

We used to pay a designer $500 to $1,000 a month. Explain the task three times, wait four days, get back the wrong thing, explain again. My design employee does that work on the first pass — and reads what I *mean* better than most people did. Three months in, we'd kept over **$3,000** in the business. From one role.

And here's what a normal Tuesday feels like now. You take a voice note on your morning walk. By the time you're home, a week of posts is written — in your voice. A lead slides into your DMs while you're with a client; your DM Closer answers, qualifies them, books the call — 60 seconds, you never touched your phone. At 7am your AI already checked your numbers and texted you the one thing that needs *you* today. You read it over coffee. That's your office now.

The first time mine wrote five posts before my coffee was cold, I just sat there. Five posts I didn't write — in my voice. That's the moment it clicked for me. That's the part that still gets me.

It does scale past the small stuff — my ad employee ran cold US traffic to about **$160,000** over eight months, and the carousels we make brought another **$5,000-plus** on top. And no, I didn't just get lucky: last year one founder, Matthew Gallagher, ran a company almost single-handedly on AI, and the number that went around was **$401 million** in his first year. Sam Altman had been saying for years that a one-person, billion-dollar company was coming. People laughed. Then it showed up.

**Now — I'm not promising you $400 million.** That would be a lie, and you'd close this video on the spot. Good instinct. Keep it. The point isn't his number, or even mine. It's that the exact tools are open to you, today. At your level, with your business, an extra one or ten thousand a month isn't a fantasy. That's what we build together — on real examples I tested myself.

---

### Beat 6 — Why this is the one bet that pays

Before we go, one thing I need you to actually hear.

Almost every skill you were told to grind on — copy, offers, ads — AI now does in seconds. A caption that used to cost you 20 minutes? Thirty seconds now. So those skills are getting cheaper by the month.

One skill goes the other way. **Up.** The skill of building the team that does the work *for* you. Picture two coaches in your niche: one ships a week of posts from a voice note on her walk, the other's still staring at a blank screen at midnight. Same hours in the day. That gap is the whole game now — and most of your competitors are still at the blank screen. That's your head start.

So here's my ask. Block the time this weekend. Protect it like a client call you can't move — because this is the client that pays you back in evenings.

---

### Beat 7 — The part you're afraid of (let me kill it now)

I know the fear, because it's the one that made me quit a course once: *"This is going to be too technical and I'll feel stupid."*

It won't. **If you can record a voice note, you can do this.** No code. No API keys. No "prompt engineering." Every single time something looks scary, I'll show you exactly where to click — and where it can trip you up, the fix is already sitting right there in the step. No googling in a panic at midnight.

Two more honest ones, so the skeptic in you can relax:

We use **Claude** in this course — but we're not affiliates. No partner links. We don't get a cent if you pick it. You could build all of this on ChatGPT or Gemini; the skill moves with you, that's the whole point. We point you to Claude for one reason: today it's the simplest place to hire a team that remembers your business and sounds like you. If that changes, I'll be the first to tell you.

So you're not learning "Claude buttons." You're learning how to build AI employees. The skill comes with you, wherever AI goes next.

---

### Beat 8 — What you've got right now

You didn't build an asset tonight. You got something you walk in with tomorrow that you didn't have today: the map, and the shift.

You know the three steps — Brain, team, company. You know the one line that runs the whole thing: *AI tells you how → AI just does it.* And you know the bet: one skill going up while the rest get cheap, and you're early enough to use it.

Tomorrow you'll look at your to-do list and see something new — not tasks, but open roles you're about to fill. Most people never get that far.

---

### Beat 9 — Your first hire

Now the one sentence.

Grab your phone or a scrap of paper. Write down **one task that drives you crazy** — the one you keep doing yourself every week even though you hate it. The one you've meant to hand off for months.

One task. One sentence. Right now. Don't overthink it.

That task is your **first AI employee.** Next lesson, you stop watching and start hiring — but first we give your AI a memory, so it knows your world before you type a single word. That's the Second Brain, and it's where everything else stands.

Welcome to your AI Dream Team. Let's get to work.
